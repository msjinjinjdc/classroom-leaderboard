Classroom Leaderboard

A lightweight, real time classroom points tracker and reward shop built with vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase.

I built this to give students a live view of weekly standings and class milestones on the projector or their own devices, while keeping point adjustments, reward redemptions, and roster management behind a PIN-protected admin mode.

What it does

Weekly Top 3 & Hall of Fame: Tracks weekly points separately from all time totals. At the end of the week, crowning the top 3 archives the podium to the Hall of Fame and resets weekly scores for a fresh start.

Reward Shop: Students can browse available classroom rewards (like candy, fruit snacks, or YouTube passes). Redeeming a reward deducts from their all-time points without hurting their weekly leaderboard standing.

Class Milestone Bar: A shared progress bar for whole class goals (e.g., earning 20 credits toward an ice cream party).

Teacher Admin Mode: Unlocked with a PIN. Lets you add custom points, award quick bonuses (+2 Brain Teaser, +1 Daily good student), manage badges, edit the reward menu, add students to the roster and filter or reassign classroom teams.
